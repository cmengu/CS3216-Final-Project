# Review notes, screens, 15 October 2026

A design critique of the Invoices screens (saved in `.impeccable/critique/`, 20 of 40 on
Nielsen's heuristics), followed by fixes to its first priority and its P1 problems, and a rebrand of the shell to
Invoice Checker. Every edit has a numbered note here. So does every point left for an owner.

Checked on this branch: `npm test` gives 192 passed; `npm run lint`, `npm run typecheck` and
`npm run build` are clean. The screens were looked at in Chrome at 1440 and 375 pixels wide,
dark and light, against a stand-in service with invented invoices (a 36-line invoice with
five problem lines, a clean one, a failed one and one still checking). The browser walks in
`scripts/` were updated but not run.

## 1. Changes made

**S-15. The overcharge box no longer says every price matched when nothing was checked.**
`overchargeBox` in `src/checker/summary.ts`. It said "None found / every price matched so far"
whenever no overcharge had been found, including when all 12 stored invoices had failed to be
read. It now says "None found" only about invoices the service checked in full, and always
names the ones it cannot speak for: "Not known yet / 12 invoices not checked in full", or
"USD 22.00 / on 1 invoice · 12 invoices not checked in full". The box is red only when an
overcharge was found. The label is "Overcharge found"; "so far" had no stated scope.

**S-16. An invoice the service would not stand behind is never "All match".** `totalKnown`
and `pileOf` in `summary.ts`. A checked invoice whose `overcharge_total` is null (the
service's sign that part of the check was uncertain, such as totals that do not reconcile)
went to "All match" when every line result was a match. It now goes to "Needs a look", labelled
"Needs a person", with the service's first warning as its reason.

**S-17. A failed invoice has its own pile.** `Pile` gains `failed`, shown as "Could not
check". Unread PDFs no longer hide a priced problem inside "Needs a look". The row label
names the step: "Could not read" (reading or extracting) or "Could not check". The rail
count is the invoices that need a look plus those that could not be checked. Until someone
picks a pile, the list opens on the first pile with anything in it.

**S-18. A failed invoice says what happened in plain words and offers a way on.**
`failure` in `summary.ts` and `Failed` in `InvoiceView.tsx`. The page used to show only
"Could not be checked. reading: OCR worker failed (exit 1)". It now leads with the step in
plain words ("The text in this PDF could not be read."), says nothing on it was checked, and
offers **Try again** and **Open the PDF**. The service's own words are under "What the
checking service reported". The header shows the status label in every state, not only
when checked.

**S-19. Try again.** `retryInvoice` in `src/checker/api.ts` reads the stored PDF from
`GET /invoices/{id}/file` and uploads it again. The service keeps the first attempt, so
`supersededBy` sets a failed attempt aside once a newer upload of the same document hash
exists, and the old page links to the newer check. `documentHash` is now carried from the
service's `document_hash`.

**S-20. Price mismatches say which way they go.** `resultLabel`: "Over sheet price" or
"Under sheet price". An undercharge card shows "Under by 2.50 each" from the service's own
difference, where it showed "Over by —". No sum is done in the browser.

**S-21. Long invoices fold their matching lines.** `rowsOf` in `InvoiceView.tsx`. While an
invoice has problems, any run of four or more matching lines folds into one row ("7 matching
lines"); a click shows every line, and "Fold the matching lines" folds them again. This
answers the layout half of SD-03.

**S-22. The problem lines stay with the paper.** Picking a card scrolls its row into view.
On wide screens the side column is sticky. J and K step through the problem lines. Esc goes
back to the list unless the assistant is open. Each marked row carries its result as
screen-reader text, not only as a tint and a letter.

**S-23. The invoice page works on a phone.** `invoices.css`. At 375 pixels the title was
about 3 pixels wide, the header labels were clipped, the problem cards came after all 36
lines, and the table scrolled sideways. The labels now wrap under the title, the problem
cards come first below 900 pixels, the paper's table fits without sideways scrolling, and the
list's boxes sit two to a row.

**S-24. Smaller fixes on the Invoices screens.**
- The fixture mark on the list is a label in the header, like on the invoice page. It was
  the faintest text on the page.
- Pile labels, box notes, the group heading and card captions use `--ink2`. `--ink3` text
  measured 3.5:1 (light) and 4.1:1 (dark), below 4.5:1.
- The dashed drop band is gone. The whole list still takes a dropped PDF and outlines itself
  while a file is dragged over it. Dropping several files says only the first is checked.
- Uploading shows "Checking file.pdf. A check can take a minute." in a live region.
- The list's error state has a Try again button. Loading shows "Loading…", not "none".
- The paper shows the year in the date and "No invoice number read" instead of "No. —".
- "Checked against" shows `price sheet 83f05608` instead of a 64-character fingerprint.
  The full fingerprint is in the tooltip.
- A clean invoice says "All 2 lines were priced against price sheet 83f05608. No overcharge
  found."

**S-25. The shell is Invoice Checker (SD-08).**
- The rail shows "Invoice Checker" and the person's role, with Invoices first.
- The order pages sit under an "Order demo" heading (`NAV_GROUPS` in
  `src/platform/nav.ts`). New tools can join the rail as another group.
- Owner and Finance land on Invoices after picking who they are (`landingFor`), and `/`
  goes to `/invoices`.
- The browser tab reads "Invoices · Invoice Checker", or the supplier and invoice number.
- "PO assistant" is "Order assistant". It still answers only about orders.
- The keyboard bar on Invoices lists only keys that work there. It advertised "↵ Open
  order", "X Select" and "↑↓ Navigate".
- The assistant's empty-state heading is an h2 (it was an h4 after the page's h1).
- The profile button has an accessible name.

**S-26. The walks follow the new words.** `scripts/e2e.mjs` and `scripts/e2e-roles.mjs`
wait for `/home` or `/invoices` after picking a person, look for "Overcharge found", count
the "Could not check" pile, and wait for `.ck-status.on` rather than any button containing
"Checking" (part of SD-04).

## 2. Left for the owner

**SD-10. Remove needs the service (Duc).** The critique asked for a way to remove a failed
upload. The service has no delete route, and `backend/api/contracts.py` changes only when
the team agrees. Until then a failed attempt drops out of the piles only when the same PDF
is uploaded again.

**SD-11. `--ink3` fails contrast across the app (Nam).** The Invoices screens no longer use
it for text that matters. The token itself is the prototype's value, and `globals.css`
changes only when the prototype does, so the rail, the keyboard bar and other screens still
use it.

**SD-12. The review step is designed, not built (Duc for storage, Nam for screens).** The
critique found that an invoice has no outcome. Nam confirmed this brief on 15 October 2026:

- The AP clerk alone decides; there is no approver. Invoices are checked as they arrive.
- Outcomes:
  - **Ready to pay** for an invoice checked in full with every line matching.
  - **Ready to pay anyway**, with a short reason, for an invoice with problems or one checked
    by hand after it could not be read.
  - **Raise with supplier** when any line is over the sheet price.
- The decision block ends the invoice page's side column. After a decision: a
  confirmation, Undo, and a link to the next invoice still to decide.
- The list's piles become **To decide** and **Done**. Rows show their outcome.
- **Dispute note:** plain text of the overcharged lines and the service's total. A Copy
  button only; nothing is sent from the app.
- **Export:** a CSV of ready-to-pay invoices by date. Columns are supplier, number, date,
  currency, total, outcome, reason, file and decision time. Fixture invoices are marked.
- Outcomes are saved **in the service**. That needs an outcome field and route in
  `backend/api/contracts.py`, which the team must agree before the screens are built.
- Built: see section 3 (S-27 to S-36). The route is proposed on its own branch and still needs
  the team (SD-14).

**SD-13. The order assistant stays in the rail.** It is named for what it does, but it
does not know about invoices.

## 3. The review step (SD-12), built

The decision step Nam confirmed in SD-12, built against the outcome route proposed in pull
request branch `nam/invoice-outcome-api` (notes B-18 to B-23 and BD-12 to BD-14 in
`2026-10-15-backend.md` on that branch). That branch changes `backend/api/contracts.py` and
waits for the team. Until it is merged, the service on `main` has no outcome route: the screens
show every invoice as To decide, and a decision says "The checking service cannot save
decisions yet."

Checked: `npm test` gives 218 passed; `npm run lint`, `npm run typecheck` and `npm run build`
are clean; the backend branch's `pytest -q` gives 128 passed and 4 skipped. The screens were
looked at in the app's browser pane at 1440 and 375 pixels wide, dark theme only (the pane
kept the app's own theme), against a stand-in service: the backend branch in fixture mode with
a reader that does no OCR, holding dummy invoices 01, 02, 03, 05, 06, 07, 08 and 10 and one PDF
that could not be read. Raise, Undo, Next, Ready to pay, Ready to pay anyway on the failed PDF,
Esc in the reason box, the Done pile and the export were each tried. No page scrolled sideways
at 375. The export was read in the page, not saved. `scripts/e2e.mjs` was updated but not run.

**S-27. An invoice carries the clerk's outcome.** `Outcome`, `outcome`, `outcomeReason` and
`decidedAt` in `src/checker/types.ts`; `toSupplierInvoice` in `convert.ts` reads them. An
outcome the screens do not know reads as no decision, never as one it might mean, and a
service from before outcomes reads as undecided.

**S-28. The outcome rules.** `choices` and `allowed` in the new `src/checker/review.ts`. The
same rules the service holds (B-20):
- **Ready to pay** only when the invoice was checked in full (`totalKnown`), has lines, and
  every line is a match. The screens' All match, so "clear" stays safe to pay.
- **Raise with supplier** only when a line is a price mismatch with a difference above zero,
  the service's own "Over sheet price".
- **Ready to pay anyway** for every finished invoice that is not clear, failed ones included,
  and only with a reason of 1 to 500 characters.
- Nothing while an invoice is still being checked.

**S-29. The decision block ends the side column.** `Decision` in `InvoiceView.tsx`. It names
why these outcomes are offered in one sentence, then shows only the buttons the rules allow.
"Ready to pay anyway…" opens a reason box; Esc in the box closes it and stays on the page (the
page's Esc no longer leaves while the box has focus). After a decision: the outcome label and
time, the reason, **Undo**, and **Next to decide** with the next invoice's supplier and number,
or "Nothing else to decide." A screen reader hears "Saved: Raise with supplier." or "Decision
undone." Coming back later, Undo reads "Change the decision". The header shows the outcome
beside the check's label. A failed PDF has the block under its panel; a failed attempt that was
sent again since has none, because it is decided on its newer check.

**S-30. The next invoice to decide.** `nextToDecide` in `review.ts`: the next undecided
invoice after this one in the list's order (newest upload first), coming round to the top. It
skips invoices still being checked and failed attempts sent again since.

**S-31. The list's piles are To decide and Done.** `Pile` and `reviewPile` in `summary.ts`,
`PILES` in `InvoicesView.tsx`. To decide is every undecided invoice, newest upload first, each
row still labelled with what the check found (SGD 15.00 over, Needs a person, Could not read,
All match, Checking…). Done is every decided invoice, newest decision first; its rows show the
outcome, the reason when there is one, and "Decided Today 10:42". The rail's Invoices count is
the To decide pile. The check's own four kinds stay as `CheckPile` and `pileOf`. A failed
attempt already decided is kept in Done after the PDF is sent again (`supersededBy`). A
decision updates every open list in place (`rememberInvoice` in `api.ts`) instead of loading
every invoice again.

**S-32. The dispute note.** `disputeNote` in `review.ts`, shown under a Raise with supplier
decision with a **Copy** button. It names the invoice, supplier and date, lists each line over
the sheet price with its quantity, both unit prices and the service's overcharge, and ends with
the service's total. Unit prices are printed to the decimals the service sent, up to six, not
rounded to four as on the cards. When the service would not stand behind the total, the note
says the total could not be confirmed and adds nothing up. Copy says "Dispute note copied.
Nothing was sent." only when the copy worked. Nothing is sent from the app.

**S-33. The export of invoices ready to pay.** `readyToPayCsv` in `review.ts`, the "Export
ready to pay (n) as CSV" button in the Done pile's heading. It holds Ready to pay and Ready to
pay anyway, sorted by invoice date, undated last, then by decision time. Columns: Supplier,
Number, Date, Currency, Total, Outcome, Reason, File, Decision time, and a tenth, **Check**,
which reads "Fixture answers, not a live check" or "Live". The total is the invoice's printed
total as the service read it. Text cells are quoted when they must be, and a cell a spreadsheet
would run as a formula (starting with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@`) gets a leading apostrophe, because
supplier names and numbers come from PDFs. The file is `ready-to-pay-<date>.csv`, built in the
browser.

**S-34. Tests.** `src/checker/review.test.ts` covers the outcome rules, the next invoice, the
dispute note and the CSV (19 tests). `summary.test.ts` covers the new piles and a decided
failed attempt; `convert.test.ts` the outcome fields; `api.test.ts` the decision call and its
error sentences. The fixture mark has a comma in it; the first CSV test caught that it was not
quoted.

**S-35. PRODUCT.md describes the outcomes.** Its Operating Context listed the four old piles.
It now lists the check's labels, the three outcomes, the To decide and Done piles, the export
and the dispute note.

**S-36. The walk follows the new piles.** `scripts/e2e.mjs` checks To decide and Done and that
the rail count is the To decide pile.

## 4. Left for the owner, after the review step

**SD-14. Merge the outcome route first (Duc and the team).** The screens need
`nam/invoice-outcome-api`. It was pushed but not opened as a pull request from here, because
the GitHub command line is not installed on this machine.

**SD-15. "By date" was read as sorted by invoice date (Nam).** The brief says "a CSV of
ready-to-pay invoices by date". The export holds every ready-to-pay invoice, oldest invoice date
first. If a payment run needs only the invoices decided since a day, that is a date filter to add.

**SD-16. The fixture mark is a tenth CSV column (Nam).** The brief lists nine columns and says
fixture invoices are marked. A mark inside another column would change what that column means,
so the export adds Check.

**SD-17. An undercharge alone reads "All match" (Nam).** `invoiceLabel` in `summary.ts` gives
dummy invoice 8 (one line under the sheet price) the label All match while its row says "1 of
3 lines needs a look". It predates the review step and is not changed here. The outcome rules
are not fooled by it: invoice 8 cannot be marked Ready to pay, only paid anyway with a reason.

**SD-18. Decisions have no history and no person (Duc).** See BD-13. Undo clears the decision;
the service keeps only the latest one.
