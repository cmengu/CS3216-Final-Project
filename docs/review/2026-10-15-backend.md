# Review notes, backend, 15 October 2026

A proposal to save the AP clerk's decision on an invoice in the service. It is the storage half
of SD-12 in `2026-10-15-screens.md`. It changes `backend/api/contracts.py`, which changes only
when the team agrees, so it is its own pull request and must not be merged until Duc and the
team have agreed the fields and the rules below. `backend/schema.py` is not changed.

Checked on this branch: `pytest -q` gives 128 passed and 4 skipped (the same four as before,
which need Docling weights or a model key). `ruff check .` and the README's
`ruff format --check` both pass.

## 1. Changes made

**B-18. A saved invoice carries an outcome.** `api/contracts.py`. `InvoiceSummary`, and so
`SavedInvoice`, gain three fields, each null until the clerk decides:

- `outcome`: `ready_to_pay`, `ready_to_pay_anyway` or `raise_with_supplier`.
- `outcome_reason`: the clerk's short reason. Required for `ready_to_pay_anyway`.
- `decided_at`: when the decision was saved, from the service's clock.

The brief named the reason field `reason`. It is `outcome_reason` here, because a summary row
already carries `error`, and a bare `reason` beside it reads as the reason the check failed.
The list returns the outcome too, so the screens can sort To decide from Done without reading
every invoice again.

**B-19. One route sets and clears it.** `PUT /invoices/{id}/outcome` with
`{"outcome": ..., "reason": ...}` in `api/app.py`. A null outcome clears the decision, which is
the screens' Undo. It answers the saved record, like `GET /invoices/{id}`. Setting a new
outcome replaces the old one; there is no history. CORS now allows `PUT`. Deciding does not
change `updated_at`, which stays the time the check last moved.

**B-20. The service holds the rules, not only the screens.** `_outcome_refusal` in
`api/app.py`. "Ready to pay" is the claim that an invoice is safe to pay, so the service
refuses it unless the screens' own All match rule holds:

| Outcome | Accepted when | Otherwise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ready_to_pay` | completed, `overcharge_total` not null, at least one line, every line `match` | 409 |
| `raise_with_supplier` | completed, and a line is `price_mismatch` with a difference above zero | 409 |
| `ready_to_pay_anyway` | completed or failed, with a reason of 1 to 500 characters after trimming | 422 without a reason |
| `null` (clear) | always; a reason sent with it is refused | 422 |

Any outcome on an invoice still being read or checked answers 409. An undercharge alone
(fixture invoice 8) cannot be raised with the supplier, since nothing is owed back; it can be
paid anyway with a reason. A failed invoice can only be paid anyway: that is the case the brief
calls "checked by hand after it could not be read".

**B-21. Older databases gain the columns.** `api/storage.py`. On start, the store adds
`outcome`, `outcome_reason` and `decided_at` to an `invoices` table that lacks them. No row is
changed. `Store.decide` writes the three columns together, so a cleared outcome never keeps an
old reason or time.

**B-22. Tests.** `tests/test_api.py`: an outcome is saved, listed and kept across a restart;
clearing it is Undo; ready to pay is refused on invoices 2, 3, 5 and 8 and on a failed read;
paying anyway needs a reason, trims it, caps it at 500 characters, works on a failed invoice and
is refused while an invoice is still being checked; raising is refused on invoices 1, 3 and 8
and accepted on 6; a database from before this change gains the columns.

**B-23. Readme.** `backend/README.md` lists the route and the rules in the HTTP contract,
marked proposed.

## 2. For the team to agree

**BD-12. Agree the contract before merging (Duc, Chen Meng).** The three fields, their names,
the route and the status codes above. The screens in SD-12 are built against exactly this
shape. `/health` still reports `contract: proposed-v1`; whether an added field is a new
contract version is the team's call.

**BD-13. No history and no person.** The outcome is the latest decision only, with no record
of who made it. PRODUCT.md says the AP clerk alone decides and there is no sign-off, and the
service has no sign-in yet, so there is no one to record. If decisions ever need an audit
trail, they belong in their own table rather than in more columns on `invoices`.

**BD-14. A decision is not undone by checking the PDF again.** A new upload of the same PDF is
a new record with no outcome; the old record keeps its decision. The screens set aside only a
failed attempt that a newer upload replaces (S-19), so a decided, completed invoice and its
re-check both stay in the list.
